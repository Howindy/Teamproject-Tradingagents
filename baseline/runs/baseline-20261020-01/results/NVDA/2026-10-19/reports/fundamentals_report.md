## 7. Key Risks & Considerations

1. **Growth Deceleration:** Revenue growth has moderated from +114-126% (FY2024-25) to +65.5% (FY2026). While still spectacular, the law of large numbers applies. The market's valuation premium may compress if growth continues to decelerate.

2. **Capital Intensity Increase:** NVIDIA is transitioning from an asset-light fabless model to heavier capital investment (capex, advanced packaging, data centers). Investing CF swung to **-$52B** in FY2026. This reduces FCF conversion relative to OCF and increases capex risk.

3. **Concentration Risk in Data Center:** The overwhelming majority of revenue now comes from data-center AI accelerators. A demand air pocket, customer inventory correction, or competitor (AMD, custom ASICs, cloud-hyperscaler in-house silicon) gaining share would be material.

4. **Export Controls / Geopolitical Risk:** The fundamental data shows immense scale built on global demand; Chinese market export restrictions remain a key geopolitical overhang for NVIDIA.

5. **Supply Chain Dependencies:** Reliance on TSMC advanced packaging/serdes and HBM suppliers (SK Hynix, Samsung, Micron) creates concentration and supply chain risk.

6. **Cyclicality:** Semiconductors are historically cyclical; the current AI-driven upcycle is unusually strong but has yet to be tested by a down-cycle at this scale.
