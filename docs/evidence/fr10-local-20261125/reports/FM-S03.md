## 8. Actionable Insights for Traders

1. **Margin durability is the standout:** 46.5% gross / 30.0% operating / 24.9% net margins signal pricing power and mix strength. Watch for sustainability into the seasonally strong December quarter.
2. **Sequential softness (-1.4% QoQ):** modest, but combined with the missing YoY comparison, traders should seek the prior-year Q3 revenue fact before drawing growth conclusions. Do not treat Q2 as a YoY proxy.
3. **Valuation:** 33.4x FY2024 EPS is a premium multiple; it prices in continued margin expansion and services growth. Any margin compression is a valuation risk.
4. **Balance-sheet leverage (80.1% liabilities/assets, 0.9x current ratio)** is structural (buybacks/debt), not a liquidity red flag per se, but limits flexibility if rates or demand shift.
5. **FCF of $72.3B YTD** underpins buybacks/dividends; the YoY CFO decline and higher capex are the key watch items.
6. **Data gap flag:** revenue_yoy_q is n/a — treat growth trajectory as unresolved until the prior-year same-quarter fact is available.
