# Apple Inc. (AAPL) — Fundamental Analysis Report
**As of 2025-08-05 | Issuer CIK 0000320193 | Fiscal year ends late September**

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Apple's most recent quarter (fiscal Q3 2025, ended 2025-06-28, filed 2025-08-01) shows a re-acceleration in top-line growth: revenue rose **9.6% YoY** to $94.04B, with gross margin at **46.5%**, operating margin at **30.0%**, and net margin at **24.9%** — all strong, high-quality profitability metrics. However, the balance sheet shows a **current ratio of 0.9x** and **liabilities-to-assets of 80.1%**, reflecting Apple's capital-return-heavy structure (large debt + buybacks) rather than distress. Year-to-date free cash flow is **$72.28B** (reported span, not a derived quarter). On valuation, the FY2024 annual P/E is **33.4x** (annual basis, not TTM). The stock closed at **$203.35** on 2025-08-04.

**Bottom line:** Fundamentals are strong and improving on growth and margins; the leverage/liquidity profile is structurally aggressive but typical for AAPL. Valuation is rich on an annual-earnings basis, so growth durability (especially the 9.6% YoY re-acceleration) is the key swing factor for traders.

## 6. Valuation

- **P/E (FY) = 33.4x**, on FY2024 annual diluted EPS of **$6.08** (FY2024: $6.08; FY2023: $6.13; FY2022: $6.11). This is an **annual** P/E, not TTM.
- Reference price: **$203.35** close on 2025-08-04 (common shares; no split/FX adjustment).
- Context: EPS was roughly flat FY2022–FY2024 ($6.11 → $6.13 → $6.08), so the 33.4x multiple is being paid for a flat-earnings base — the bull case rests on the FY2025 re-acceleration (Q3 revenue +9.6% YoY) translating into renewed EPS growth.

## 7. Actionable Insights for Traders

1. **Growth inflection is the key catalyst.** Q3 FY2025 revenue +9.6% YoY vs. FY2024's +2.0% annual growth signals a meaningful acceleration. Watch whether this persists into the September quarter (FY Q4) — a deceleration would pressure the 33.4x annual multiple.
2. **Margin resilience supports earnings quality.** 46.5% gross / 30.0% operating / 24.9% net margins in Q3 are strong; any mix shift toward lower-margin hardware or tariff/component cost pressure would be the main margin risk to monitor.
3. **Liquidity/leverage is structural, not acute.** Current ratio 0.9x and liabilities-to-assets 80.1% are typical of Apple's buyback-funded capital structure; traders should treat these as capital-allocation signals rather than solvency red flags, but they do reduce the balance-sheet cushion if cash flow weakens.
4. **Cash generation remains the anchor.** $72.28B YTD FCF (reported span) funds buybacks/dividends; the YoY decline in operating cash flow ($81.75B vs. $91.44B) alongside rising capex ($9.47B vs. $6.54B) is worth tracking for FCF trajectory.
5. **Valuation is full.** At 33.4x FY2024 earnings with flat FY2022–FY2024 EPS, the stock prices in a return to growth. Position sizing should reflect execution risk on the growth re-acceleration.
